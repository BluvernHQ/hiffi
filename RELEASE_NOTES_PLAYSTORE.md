# Hiffi – Release notes (v1.0.0 – Play Store)

Use the **short** version for the Play Store "What's new" field (max 500 characters). Use the **full** version for release notes or in-app changelog if needed.

---

## Short (for Play Store "What's new" – under 500 chars)

**What's new in 1.0.0**

• Redesigned video player controls – center play/pause, previous & next video buttons  
• Swipe up on the video to go fullscreen (YouTube-style)  
• Previous video button now goes back to the last video; disabled when there’s no history  
• Cleaner thumbnails – view count removed; processing badge only when needed  
• Video no longer goes under the status bar – safe area with black bars  
• Smoother loading – blurred thumbnail while the video loads, no spinner overlay  

---

## Full (optional – for blog, in-app, or extended release notes)

**Video player**

- **New controls:** Center play/pause only; previous and next video buttons replace seek buttons. Use double-tap on the left/right of the video to seek backward/forward.
- **Pull to fullscreen:** In portrait, swipe up on the video to enter fullscreen (landscape).
- **Previous video:** Tap previous to go back to the last watched video. Button is disabled when there’s no previous video (e.g. when you open from the feed).
- **Safe area:** Video stays below the status bar; letterbox area is black.
- **Loading:** Blurred thumbnail while the stream loads; loading spinner overlay removed for a cleaner look.

**Thumbnails**

- View count overlay removed from video thumbnails across the app.
- “Processing” badge still shown only when a video is processing.
